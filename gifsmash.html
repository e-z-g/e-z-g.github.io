Ordered dithering compresses far better than error diffusion — the pattern repeats, so LZW eats it. Higher scale means a weaker, coarser pattern.

Needed for sources with alpha, and for -O2/-O3 to leave unchanged pixels out of a frame. Costs one colour.

Loop behaviour
% under target — 0 runs every iteration

The script divided every weight by 2 no matter what the others were, so weight 1 means "close half the gap". Sharing divides by the sum of the four instead, which is what the line it overwrote was computing. As-given divides by nothing: in threshold mode that is the setting where weight 1 walks a knob all the way to its limit by the last iteration. In approximate mode it applies the whole correction at once, which overshoots and then swings back — useful for finding the floor fast, not for landing on a number.
